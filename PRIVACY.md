# Route My Showers: Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 6, 2026_

Route My Showers is a browser extension that draws your bike routes on the Warmshowers host map.

## What it stores
- The routes you add (name, track points, distance, and the link or file name they came from) and your settings (units, distance markers, show/hide).
- All of it is kept in your browser's local extension storage on your device. It is not synced and not sent anywhere.

## What it sends
- When you add a route link, the extension downloads that route straight from the site it came from (RideWithGPS, Komoot or Strava), using your existing login on that site if needed.
- The extension has no server of its own. It does not collect analytics, does not track you, and does not share or sell any data.
- Clipboard text is read only when you press **Paste & add**, and only to find a route link.

## Removing your data
Remove routes from the panel, or uninstall the extension to delete everything it stored.
